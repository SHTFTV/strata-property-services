Why this works for your "Industry Army" model:
The "Visual Tease": The left side uses CSS to mimic a document. It shows "PASS" and "0 PPM CO"—the two things a Strata Manager cares most about—without them having to read a word.

The Lead Magnet: The "Download Sample" button is your primary hook. Once they see how detailed your reporting is compared to a handwritten receipt, they can't go back to their old contractor.

Asset Management: Mentioning "Serial #s" is a secret weapon. Most Stratas don't have an inventory of their units. By providing one, you become an indispensable partner for their next Depreciation Report.
